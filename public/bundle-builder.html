How do colours and stock work?

One row is one component choice. If a row says quantity 1 and allows red, blue and green grinders, the deal contains one of those grinders—not all three.

Choose Product · all colours for normal Shopify variants. Choose SKU family when colours are separate products, for example BOX_1_ includes BOX_1_RED and BOX_1_BLUE. New matching items qualify automatically. Choose Specific item for a fixed SKU or a differently priced option.

At the till, tap Build a deal in Shopify POS. Choose or scan the colours, check the complete set, then add it at the deal price. Items already scanned are reused. The till app uses this saved bundle and its existing Shopify product's price. Shopify deducts the actual components when checkout is completed. Saving here does not change stock or update Bundles.app.
