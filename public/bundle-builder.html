Where does the kit exist, and how do colours work?

The completed kit is an existing product and SKU in Shopify, such as Crown Rolling Kit / Black. A recipe links to that kit and describes its contents. Your saved tests live only in this app; they do not record a completed assembly in Shopify.

One recipe can allow every colour of a component product. You do not need a separate recipe for each combination. Choose the actual colour used so its stock and cost can be checked, even when all colours have the same selling price.

You can reuse a recipe for many batches. The number of kits you could make depends on the available component stock at your chosen store. Test mode does not reserve that stock.

What goes in each kit

For interchangeable colours, choose All variants of one product. This includes that product's variants without adding them one by one. Use a specific SKU or a selected list when only certain items belong in this kit.

Saved test previews

Each test records who saved it, the kit recipe, store, quantities, costs and actual items chosen. These records are saved in this app, not in Shopify. No stock has been added, reserved or deducted.

Export CSV

CSV exports the full preview history for Microsoft Access. It is a planning record, not an inventory adjustment import.
